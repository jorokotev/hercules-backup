Privacy policy

hercules-backup is used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google Drive solely to store, check and restore the owner's backups.

It does not collect, share, sell or transfer any data to anyone. No analytics or tracking. All data stays in the owner's own Google Drive and on the owner's own computer.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
